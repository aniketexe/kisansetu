The Process

Simple steps, real impact

From application to disbursement, every step is guided, verified, and transparent.

STEP 01
📝

Farmer Applies

Farmers submit a simple loan request — crop type, amount needed, repayment period. NGO field workers can assist with data entry in regional languages.

STEP 02
🔍

NGO Reviews & Verifies

NGO staff verify land records, assess need, and approve the application. Approved loans are listed publicly for donors to browse and fund.

STEP 03
💰

Donors Fund the Loan

Individual or institutional donors browse farmer profiles and contribute any amount. Progress bars show live funding status in real-time.

STEP 04
🚜

Funds Disbursed

Once fully funded, the NGO disburses funds directly to the farmer via bank transfer or cash. The farmer receives a repayment schedule.

STEP 05
📊

Transparent Tracking

Donors receive repayment updates and impact reports. Farmers can view their loan status, upcoming payments, and history anytime.

STEP 06
🔄

Repayment & Renewal

Repaid funds can be re-lent to new farmers, creating a revolving pool of community capital that compounds over time.

Who It's For

A platform built for everyone

Three distinct portals, each designed around the needs of its users.

👨‍🌾

Farmers

Landless or small-scale farmers who need quick, dignified access to crop and equipment loans without complex paperwork.

  • Submit loan applications in minutes
  • Track funding progress live
  • Receive repayment reminders
  • View full loan history
  • Support in regional languages
🏢

NGO / Admin

The backbone of the platform — NGO staff manage applications, verify farmers, and ensure funds reach the right hands.

  • Application review dashboard
  • Farmer verification tools
  • Disbursement management
  • Reports and impact analytics
  • Donor relationship tools
💚

Donors

Individuals and organizations who want to make a direct, measurable difference in a farmer's livelihood and food security.

  • Browse verified farmer profiles
  • Fund any amount, any loan
  • Track repayment in real-time
  • Download tax receipts
  • Monthly impact reports
